# Ticket Policy

> Fictional demo content for this public prototype.

The registration assistant supports exactly three ticket types: `student`, `professional`, and `vip`.

Each attendee must select exactly one ticket type.

## Student Ticket (`student`)

The student ticket costs 49 USD.

It is intended for students, bootcamp learners, and early-career attendees with a valid student email or student ID.

Included:

- Conference access for selected days
- Keynotes
- Expo hall
- One workshop preference

Not included:

- VIP lounge
- Speaker dinner

## Professional Ticket (`professional`)

The professional ticket costs 149 USD.

It is intended for engineers, researchers, founders, product managers, designers, and working professionals.

Included:

- Conference access for selected days
- Keynotes
- Expo hall
- One workshop preference
- Saturday networking reception

Not included:

- VIP lounge
- Speaker dinner

## VIP Ticket (`vip`)

The VIP ticket costs 299 USD.

It is intended for attendees who want premium networking access and reserved seating.

Included:

- Conference access for selected days
- Reserved keynote seating
- VIP lounge
- Speaker dinner
- One workshop preference

## Day Selection

A ticket is valid only for the days selected during registration. Attendees may select one, two, or three days from `june_12`, `june_13`, and `june_14`.

Single-day discount pricing is not offered. Coupon codes are not available through the registration assistant.
