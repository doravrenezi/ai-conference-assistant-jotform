# Conference Overview

> Fictional demo content for this public prototype.

Demo AI Conference 2026 is a three-day AI conference for students, engineers, researchers, founders, and product teams. The conference focuses on practical AI systems, automation, retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents, prompt engineering, and responsible deployment.

The conference takes place at Demo Conference Center in San Francisco, California. All sessions use the America/Los_Angeles timezone.

## Dates

The valid conference days are:

| Canonical ID | Display date | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `june_12` | Friday, June 12, 2026 | AI Foundations and Product Strategy |
| `june_13` | Saturday, June 13, 2026 | Agents, Automation, and Applied RAG |
| `june_14` | Sunday, June 14, 2026 | Responsible Deployment and Future Work |

Attendees may select one, two, or all three conference days during registration.

## Registration Scope

Registration requires full name, email, company or university, role or title, ticket type, selected conference days, workshop preference, dietary preference, and optional accessibility needs.

Registration is created only after the attendee reviews the collected information and confirms submission.

## Valid Ticket Types

The valid ticket type IDs are `student`, `professional`, and `vip`.

## Valid Workshops

The valid workshop IDs are `ai_agents`, `rag_basics`, and `prompt_engineering`.
