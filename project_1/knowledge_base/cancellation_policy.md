# Cancellation Policy

> Fictional demo content for this public prototype.

Registrations for Demo AI Conference 2026 can have the lifecycle status `active` or `cancelled`.

Cancelling a registration keeps the original registration record and registration code for audit, support, and attendee lookup purposes. A cancelled registration is no longer valid for conference check-in, badge pickup, workshops, networking events, or VIP benefits.

## How to Cancel

Attendees can request cancellation through the registration assistant. For security, the assistant must verify both:

- Registration code
- Email address used for registration

After the registration is found, the assistant will summarize the registration details and ask for final confirmation before changing the status to `cancelled`.

## Cancellation Deadlines

The cancellation deadline depends on when the request is confirmed:

| Cancellation confirmed | Refund eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| On or before May 15, 2026 at 23:59 Pacific Time | Full refund, excluding payment processing fees |
| May 16 through May 31, 2026 at 23:59 Pacific Time | 50% refund, excluding payment processing fees |
| June 1, 2026 or later | Not eligible for refund |

Refunds are returned to the original payment method when possible. Processing may take 5 to 10 business days after the cancellation is confirmed.

## No-Show Policy

If an attendee does not check in for any selected conference day and does not cancel before the applicable deadline, the registration is treated as a no-show. No-show registrations are not eligible for refunds.

## Ticket Transfers

Registrations may be transferred to another attendee until June 5, 2026 at 23:59 Pacific Time. Transfer requests require the original registration code, the original attendee email, and the new attendee's full name and email address.

Workshop preferences and selected conference days remain attached to the transferred registration unless the new attendee updates them before transfer processing is completed.

## Workshop and VIP Benefits

Cancelling a registration releases any reserved workshop seat. For VIP ticket holders, cancellation also removes access to reserved keynote seating, the VIP lounge, and the speaker dinner.

## Cancelled Registrations

A cancelled registration cannot be used for check-in. If an attendee wants to attend after cancelling, they must create a new registration, subject to ticket availability and workshop capacity.

If a cancelled registration is looked up later, the registration status appears as `cancelled`.

## Registration Records

The conference registration system keeps an internal registration record and syncs confirmed changes to the connected Jotform submission.

When a registration is cancelled, the internal registration record is updated first. The system then attempts to update the related Jotform submission status to `cancelled`.

If the Jotform update fails, the registration remains cancelled in the conference registration system. The attendee receives a clear message explaining that the cancellation was saved but could not be synced to Jotform.

## Local Records

Cancellation does not delete the registration record. The registration code, attendee email, selected days, ticket type, workshop preference, and cancellation status remain stored so the support team can verify the request later.
