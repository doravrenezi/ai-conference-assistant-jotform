# FAQ

> Fictional demo content for this public prototype.

## What is the conference called?

The conference is called Demo AI Conference 2026.

## What dates can I select?

The valid day IDs are `june_12`, `june_13`, and `june_14`. They correspond to Friday, June 12, Saturday, June 13, and Sunday, June 14, 2026.

## Can I attend only one day?

Yes. Attendees may select one, two, or all three conference days.

## What ticket types are available?

The valid ticket types are `student`, `professional`, and `vip`.

## What workshops are available?

The valid workshop preferences are `ai_agents`, `rag_basics`, and `prompt_engineering`.

## Can I choose more than one workshop?

No. Each registration includes exactly one workshop preference.

## Can I change my registration later?

Yes. Updating an existing registration requires the registration code and the email address used for registration.

## Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. Cancelling an existing registration requires the registration code and the email address used for registration. The cancellation request is summarized for final confirmation before the registration status changes.

## Are meals included?

Lunch is scheduled for all three conference days. The Saturday networking reception is included with `professional` and `vip` tickets. The speaker dinner is included only with the `vip` ticket.

## Which dietary preferences are supported?

Supported dietary preferences include no preference, vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, and allergy notes. Attendees may also provide a short free-text dietary note during registration.

## Are hotel discounts available?

Partner hotel discounts are not available through the registration assistant. Attendees should book accommodations directly with their preferred hotel.

## Are parking prices available?

Limited paid parking is available in the Demo Garage next to the venue for 28 USD per day, subject to availability.
