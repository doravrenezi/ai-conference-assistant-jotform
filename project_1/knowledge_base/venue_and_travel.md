# Venue and Travel

> Fictional demo content for this public prototype.

Demo AI Conference 2026 takes place at Demo Conference Center, 100 Example Avenue, San Francisco, CA.

All sessions use the America/Los_Angeles timezone.

## Check-in

Attendees should check in at the main lobby registration desk. A government-issued ID or student ID may be requested for badge pickup.

Check-in windows:

| Day ID | Date | Check-in window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `june_12` | Friday, June 12, 2026 | 08:00-17:00 |
| `june_13` | Saturday, June 13, 2026 | 08:30-17:30 |
| `june_14` | Sunday, June 14, 2026 | 09:00-15:30 |

## Transportation

The venue is a 10-minute walk from the Example transit stop. Ride-share pickup is available at the north entrance.

## Parking

Limited paid parking is available in the Demo Garage next to the venue. Parking is 28 USD per day, subject to availability.

## Accessibility

The venue has step-free entry, elevator access, accessible restrooms, and reserved seating areas. Attendees can provide accessibility needs during registration.

## Hotels

Partner hotel discounts are not available through the registration assistant. Attendees should book accommodations directly with their preferred hotel.
